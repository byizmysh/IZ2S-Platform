IZ2S Cardputer ADV application 0.21

Application source follows the IZ2S repository license (see root LICENSE).
The binary is built from src/main.cpp and the pinned platformio.ini dependencies.
The build recipe is build.py. Credentials are loaded from SD and are not compiled in.
