IZ2S 0.3.5
Author of integration: IZMYSH
Console launcher: 0.3.5
For Linux x64 and ARM64.
1. Extract IZ2S-0.3.5-linux.zip on your PC and open the IZ2S-0.3.5-linux folder.
2. Install and open the panel from that folder with sh ./install.sh --launch.
3. Extract the contents of iz2sciv-core-sd.zip next to install.sh to the card root. Check that SD:/iz2s/ exists directly on the card.
See README.md, README.ru.md and docs/INSTALLATION.md.
