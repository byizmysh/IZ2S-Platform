# Authors and acknowledgements

See the Authors section in [README.md](README.md) for the principal projects and maintainers. [LICENSES/copyrights.json](LICENSES/copyrights.json) preserves the additional copyright and SPDX lines found in shipped source and notices, with their locations. [LICENSES/npm-components.json](LICENSES/npm-components.json) records names, authors, contributors, versions and licenses declared by bundled JavaScript packages. Full upstream notices take precedence over this summary.

No attribution is removed merely because its original project name differs from IZ2S. Firmware notices, Electron/Chromium notices, fonts, native library notices and module-specific licenses remain with their owners.
